# Cement Demand Forecasting Across Multiple Construction Sites

> **Business Intelligence Case Study | Construction Industry**

---

# Project Overview

This project was completed for a construction company to help improve how cement demand was monitored across multiple construction sites.

The company had large volumes of operational data but lacked a centralised reporting solution that could quickly answer important business questions. My role was to transform that data into meaningful insights through Business Intelligence dashboards that support planning, inventory management, and operational decision-making.

Rather than simply building dashboards, the focus was on understanding the business problem, identifying the information decision-makers needed most, and presenting those insights in a clear and practical way.

---

# The Challenge

The company needed a better way to monitor cement demand across different construction sites.

Without a centralised reporting solution, it was difficult to:

- Monitor cement consumption across sites.
- Compare deliveries with actual demand.
- Track inventory levels.
- Identify operational trends.
- Support future demand forecasting.

Management needed a solution that could turn operational data into information that could be understood and acted upon quickly.

---

# My Approach

To address this challenge, I:

- Extracted operational data from the company's SQLite database using SQL.
- Cleaned and transformed the data using Power Query.
- Built an analytical data model in Power BI.
- Created DAX measures to calculate business KPIs.
- Designed interactive dashboards for different business users.
- Analysed operational performance and identified key trends.
- Presented practical recommendations based on the findings.

---

# The Solution

The final solution was an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard that enables stakeholders to monitor:

- Cement consumption across construction sites.
- Cement deliveries.
- Inventory levels.
- Silo utilisation.
- Regional performance.
- Demand trends over time.

The dashboards provide management with a faster and more effective way to monitor operations and make informed decisions.

---

# Business Impact

The project helped transform raw operational data into meaningful business information.

The solution enables decision-makers to:

- Monitor cement demand more efficiently.
- Improve inventory planning.
- Identify unusual consumption patterns.
- Reduce manual reporting.
- Support future demand forecasting.
- Make faster, data-driven business decisions.
